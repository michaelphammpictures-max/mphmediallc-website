What makes video perform

Six things that separate video that works from video that gets ignored.

Good video isn't just about the camera. It's about the message. Here's what we focus on in every project.

  • 01

    Start with a real customer question

    The best videos answer something people are already asking. That's what gets searched, watched, and remembered.

  • 02

    Sound like a person

    Talk the way you'd talk to a customer across the table. Polished is good. Stiff is not.

  • 03

    Hook early

    Social clips have seconds to earn attention. We open on the strongest moment, not the slow lead-in.

  • 04

    Add captions

    Plenty of people scroll with the sound off. Captions keep your message working even when no one hears it.

  • 05

    One idea per clip

    A clip that makes one clear point beats a clip that tries to say everything.

  • 06

    Always give a next step

    Every video should tell viewers what to do next, whether that's calling, booking, or watching the full video.

Questions

Good questions. Straight answers.

What do I get from one shoot?

Full-length videos for your website and YouTube, plus short clips cut for Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook. The exact amount depends on your project, and we'll go over it on the discovery call.

How does a transcript help me get found?

Search engines can't watch video, but they can read text. A transcript turns what you say into words that match what customers search for. It helps you show up, though no one can promise a specific ranking.

Do you handle the editing too?

Yes. We handle the full process, from strategy and filming to editing and delivery.

Why do I need both long-form videos and short clips?

They do different jobs. Short clips grab attention on social media. Long-form video builds trust and gives search engines something to read. Together, they bring people in and help them decide.

How soon will I see results?

Video builds over time. Search visibility grows as you keep publishing, and social clips help keep you in front of people in the meantime. We'll set honest expectations with you up front.
